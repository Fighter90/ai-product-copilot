# Language and theme

## Interface language

The RU/EN switch is in the cabinet header and on public pages. The choice persists in the browser (localStorage) and applies to every screen: tables, charts, help and the assistant.

## Theme

Dark and light themes are toggled with the sun/moon button. The choice persists too. ECharts charts recolor automatically.

## Number and date formats

With RU selected, dates use DD.MM.YYYY and thousands are space-separated. With EN — English rules apply.
